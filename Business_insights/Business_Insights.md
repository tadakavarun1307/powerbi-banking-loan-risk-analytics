# Business Insights – Banking Loan Portfolio & Customer Risk Analysis

## 1. Portfolio Overview

The analysis evaluates a banking loan portfolio using SQL, Power BI, DAX, and Power Query to understand loan performance, customer risk, repayment behavior, and regional trends.

Key portfolio metrics identified through the Power BI dashboard include:

- Total Customers: 5K
- Total Loans: 8K
- Total Loan Amount: 504M
- Total Payments: 25.27M
- Average Loan Amount: 63.06K

## 2. Customer Risk Analysis

Customer segmentation was used to compare loan exposure across different customer risk segments.

The analysis helps identify:

- Customer segments with higher loan exposure
- Segments requiring closer credit monitoring
- Differences in loan behavior across customer groups
- Opportunities for targeted customer retention strategies

## 3. Loan Status Analysis

Loan status analysis provides visibility into the distribution of approved, rejected, and other loan outcomes.

This helps banking teams:

- Monitor approval and rejection patterns
- Identify potential credit-risk trends
- Evaluate portfolio quality
- Improve lending decisions

## 4. Regional Analysis

Regional loan analysis was used to compare portfolio activity across geographic areas.

Regional comparisons can help identify:

- High-performing regions
- Regions with higher loan exposure
- Differences in customer behavior
- Opportunities for regional lending strategies

## 5. Interest Rate Analysis

Average interest rates were analyzed across customer risk segments.

This can help the bank understand:

- How pricing varies across customer segments
- Whether higher-risk customers carry different interest rates
- Potential opportunities for risk-based pricing
- The relationship between customer risk and loan profitability

## 6. Key Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, banking teams can:

1. Prioritize monitoring of high-risk customer segments.
2. Review loan approval and rejection patterns to improve credit decisions.
3. Use regional performance to optimize lending strategies.
4. Apply risk-based pricing where appropriate.
5. Monitor loan defaults and repayment behavior regularly.
6. Use Power BI dashboards for ongoing portfolio monitoring.

## 7. Business Value

This project demonstrates how banking data can be transformed into actionable insights using:

- SQL for data analysis
- DAX for business metrics
- Power BI for interactive visualization
- Power Query for data transformation
- Business analysis for decision-making

The dashboard provides a centralized view of loan portfolio performance and customer risk, helping stakeholders make data-driven lending and portfolio-management decisions.
